# Coen Hao

Physics student at the University of Toronto. I love controls, ESP32 stuff, and cars. Most of my projects end up being some mix of a microcontroller, a 3D printer, and something that moves.

**Portfolio:** [refriderator.github.io/Portfolio](https://refriderator.github.io/Portfolio/)

## What I'm into

- **Controls:** PID loops, state machines, closed-loop boost and AFR on a Haltech, stabilization on an ESP32.
- **Embedded:** ESP32 and Arduino firmware, UART, PWM, sensors, wiring harnesses.
- **Robotics:** room navigation and SLAM with Kinect depth cameras, a local voice assistant.
- **Vehicle dynamics:** suspension geometry, setup, and logged data, from Formula Student to competition drifting.
- **3D printing & fabrication:** airframes, intake prototypes, jigs, uprights.
- **RC:** fixed-wing planes designed in xflr5, running my own flight firmware.
