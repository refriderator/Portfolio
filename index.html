Turbocharged Miata: Full Engine Build

Team Opposite Lock competition car

End-to-end forced-induction build on OEM architecture. Mazdaspeed Miata oil pan, turbo hot side, and intake piping combined with an NB2 Miata 10:1 compression VVT block and top end. Street and dyno tuned.

  • Designed and budgeted the whole system, then specced and installed fuel, intake, and exhaust.
  • Designed and built the oil and coolant feed and return lines, plus a data logging system.
  • Tuned on a Haltech standalone ECU with a 3-port boost control solenoid and an aftermarket recirculation valve: closed-loop AFR and boost control, PID idle tuning, and idle diagnosis.
  • Adapted the downpipe to the standard Miata exhaust mounting location, welded the EGR system shut, and reused the Mazdaspeed oil catch can.
  • Performed all wiring and signal diagnosis, including isolating idle faults across mechanical, electrical, and software causes, and identifying and RMA’ing an ECU board with a bad spark signal.
  • Validated the build on a 1,500-mile road trip the day after completion, plus 8+ hours of competition driving.

Skills: powertrain integration, standalone ECU tuning, electrical diagnosis, systems validation.

Front Suspension Geometry: More Steering Lock

Applied vehicle dynamics on the competition car

Moved the tie-rod mounting location on the knuckle to increase lock-to-lock steering angle, then corrected the geometry effects that came with it.

  • Increased steering lock by 20° by relocating the tie-rod mount on the knuckle.
  • Corrected the resulting Ackermann and bump steer changes, made worse by the lowered ride height, by building 2 cm steering rack spacers specced from basic geometry simulations.
  • Made decisions from in-car measurement, Assetto Corsa simulator data, suspension-geometry tools, and OEM Mazda documentation.

Skills: suspension kinematics, geometry analysis, sim-validated design.

Double Wishbone Suspension Simulator: MX-5 NB Kinematics Study

Four-corner, 7-degree-of-freedom model in JavaScript

Built a four-corner, 7-degree-of-freedom double wishbone model of the MX-5 NB in JavaScript, with geometry taken from measured arms, knuckles, and subframe pickup points. Used it to compare spring, roll bar, and ride height changes before touching the car.

  • Modeled the front and rear linkages from traced control arms and pickup drawings, then validated the stock car against Mazda’s published track, roll center heights, kingpin angle, caster trail, and full steering lock.
  • Compared stock springs against two coilover setups: 8 / 6 kgf/mm cuts roll from 5.7 to 3.1 °/g and shifts roll stiffness rearward.
  • Analyzed ride height and roll center movement: lowering 26 mm front / 38 mm rear drops the front roll center from 41 to 3 mm but only lengthens the roll lever by about 5 mm.
  • Quantified camber from a shortened front upper arm (about 0.25° per mm) and its side effects on kingpin angle and bump steer.
  • Found and fixed a geometry fit that matched the published numbers for the wrong reasons, by replacing photo-scaled guesses with measured dimensions.

Skills: vehicle dynamics, suspension kinematics, model validation, JavaScript, three.js, data analysis.

Team OL

Team Opposite Lock

Competition team-tandem drifting · 2021 – present

Team Opposite Lock was formed in 2021 by a small group of high school seniors from Detroit. Today, we are the youngest team competing at the highest level of international team-tandem drift competition.

The Team

Team Opposite Lock competes at the top tier of international tandem drifting against teams from Japan, Australia, Europe, and South America, many with far larger budgets and decades more experience.

My Rôle

  • Driver. Competition driver at Final Bout Special Stage Central 2025, translating simulator and real-world telemetry into car setup and driving adjustments, closing following distance, and maximizing performance against drastically different car architectures and setups.
  • Vehicle & tire tech. Fitment, competition scrutineering, and run plan strategy built from tire wear, performance, and temperature data for the specific track and conditions on any given event day. Damper, spring rate, and ride height tuning; maintaining roll center and center of gravity after modifications; contact patch management through bushing and suspension changes and live adjustment.
  • Fabrication & maintenance. Fiberglass patching and forming, weatherproofing, and NVH work; visual scrutineering. Track day quick repairs and setup changes; maintaining vehicle integrity and executing emergency repairs under time and materials constraints.
  • Operations & business. Managed the team budget, sponsorship relations and professional communications, shop financials, and merchandising. Planning and logistics for events, including international competition, and trackside operations.
  • Community. Social media, videography, and content creation. Building a global community with a strong presence across the West Coast, the Midwest US, and Southeast Asia. Built and sold Assetto Corsa car/track 3D assets and tire-physics models to independent artists and drivers, scanned and modeled from the real world.
